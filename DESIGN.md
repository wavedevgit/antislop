# Design

## Documentation surface

- Mode: Read.
- Soft slate background and panels with restrained blue accents and low-glare off-white text.
- Sometype Sans for interface labels and prose; Roboto Mono only for code, commands, and scan output.
- Fixed 56px top bar with the AntiSlop logo and source link.
- 264px rule sidebar with grouped navigation and a persistent search field.
- Content measure of 840px with hairline section rules and generous vertical spacing.
- Home opens with the product claim and two actions in a distinct, divided hero section, followed by a real scanner finding.
- Rule pages keep screenshots full-width inside a hairline frame and use compact, borderless tables.
- Mobile collapses the sidebar behind a 56px toggle and stacks command rows.
