# GPT-5.6 Luna test results

GPT-5.6 Luna generated five independent pages in `experiment/luna/`. AntiSlop was then run against that batch, and the repeated-pattern analysis is saved in `experiment/luna/repeated-designs.md`.

The batch triggered: Unicode bullets, cream colors, text arrows, em dashes, and emojis.

The strongest uncovered patterns were:

- boxed/panelized content modules: 5/5 pages
- hero-led page structure: 5/5 pages
- grid/flex-heavy composition: 5/5 pages
- repeated utility labels: 4/5 pages
