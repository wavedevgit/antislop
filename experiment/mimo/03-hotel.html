Nine rooms above the harbour.

Hotel Zephyr sits on the sea wall of Portlan, two doors down from the fish market and one gust from the pier. Breakfast at eight, boot rack by the door, and a tide chart in every room.

The rooms

Every room faces water. The ones on the third floor take the weather hardest and are priced accordingly.

The road past the harbour closes in high winter storms. If you are driving in after dark, ring the desk and we will talk you round the front.

Around Portlan

The fish market opens at six, which is louder than you would think. The coast path starts at the end of the sea wall and reaches the lighthouse in about ninety minutes at an unhurried pace.

In summer there is a brass band on the pier on alternate Saturdays. In winter there is mostly weather, which we think is the better show.
