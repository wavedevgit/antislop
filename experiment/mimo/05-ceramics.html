A working ceramics studio on Bexhill Lane.

Twelve wheels, two kilns, one extremely patient glaze librarian. We teach beginners, take in work to fire, and open the membership shelves to anyone willing to wash their own splash pans.

How a pot gets made here

Throw or build

Wheels and handbuilding tables are booked by the two-hour slot.

Dry on the rack

Leather-hard work waits on labelled shelves near the damp box.

Bisque fire

Kiln 1 goes to 1000°C most Wednesdays when it is full enough.

Glaze & fire

Glaze day is Saturday. Work returns to your shelf three weeks later.
