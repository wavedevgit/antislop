Est. 2011 · Millbrook Neighbourhood

Twelve garden beds, one very muddy community.

We are a volunteer-run club tending shared plots on a reclaimed corner lot between the tram depot and Kestrel Creek. Tomatoes, arguments about compost, and a shed with a questionable padlock since 2011.

Become a member

What's growing where

The lot is divided into twelve plots plus a communal herb spiral and a pollinator strip along the creek side. Each plot is allocated by ballot every March.

PLOTS 1–4

Brassica row

Kale, savoy cabbage, and the cauliflower that always wins the September show. Watered by hose from the depot standpipe.

PLOTS 5–8

Tomato houses

Two polytunnels held together with tape and optimism. Members share the harvest rota; nobody may take more than four fruit per week.

PLOTS 9–12

Orchard & soft fruit

Three dwarf apples, a quince, and an aggressive blackberry that has eaten the west fence twice.

Join the club

Membership is 18 € a year, which covers water, seed bulk buys, and the annual plot supper. No experience required; enthusiasm and closed-toe shoes are.
