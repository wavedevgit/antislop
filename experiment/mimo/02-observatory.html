Thursday public night

Doors 19:30, first light 20:00, hot cocoa in the warm room until we lock up around 23:00. No booking needed for individuals; groups of ten or more should email first so we can staff the second scope.

Getting here

The last 4 km of ridge road is unpaved; take it slowly after rain. Park at the lower lot by the transmitter mast — the dome road is reserved for accessibility permits. Red torches only past the gate.
