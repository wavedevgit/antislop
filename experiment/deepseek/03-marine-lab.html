What we're learning

Three questions,
one stretch of coast.

Long-term monitoring is unglamorous and essential. These are the threads our team is pulling right now, with partners up and down the Sound.

PROGRAMME 01

Kelp recovery

Bull kelp has thinned by nearly two thirds since 2015. We are mapping the surviving beds, growing local stock, and testing small replantings with the dive team.

−62% since 2015 Canopy extent, 14 survey sites
PROGRAMME 02

Orca acoustics

Six hydrophones listen around the clock. We publish call detections within the hour so ferry captains and whale-watch crews can slow down where it matters.

6 hydrophones · 24/7 1,140 calls identified this year
PROGRAMME 03

Microplastics

From surface slicks to mussel beds, we are counting what shouldn't be there. The first five years of samples are now open for other labs to reuse.

1,200 samples Open dataset · updated quarterly

At sea

The Petrel goes
out on the tide.

A 14-metre converted fishing boat, crew of four, bunks for six. We run day trips for volunteers and week-long transects for visiting researchers. No experience needed, sea legs helpful.
