From Tuesday night to Wednesday morning

Thirty-six hours,
mostly waiting.

There is no shortcut worth taking. The starter does the work; we just keep it warm and pay attention. Every loaf is mixed, folded, and shaped by hand.

0hours

Mix & rest

Stoneground flour, water, salt, starter. Nothing else, nothing rushed.

6hours

Fold

Four sets, an hour apart. The dough tells you when it's ready.

24hours

Shape & chill

Overnight in the cold room. This is where the flavour comes from.

36hours

Bake

250°C, plenty of steam, twenty-five minutes. Then a long rest.
