How it works.

Three steps, one phone call, and a bike that stops when you tell it to.

01

Bring it in

Walk in with a flat or book ahead for bigger work. We'll look at it while you're there and give you a written estimate.

02

We call first

If we find something else, you get a call before we spend a cent. No "while we were in there" surprises.

03

Ride away

Every bike gets a test ride and a torque check before pickup. If it doesn't feel right, bring it back.

The bench · since 1998
"A bike is not a luxury. It's how you get to work."

Rueben opened the shop with one stand, a truing jig, and a borrowed kettle. Twenty-eight years later there are four stands, two mechanics, and still the same kettle. We fix what's broken and leave the rest alone.
