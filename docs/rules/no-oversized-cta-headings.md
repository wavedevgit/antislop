# Oversized CTA headings

![Oversized CTA headings example](./assets/no-oversized-cta-headings.png)

AI-generated landing pages often pair a very large heading with a call to action.

## How it works

Finds unusually large headings used next to calls to action.

## How to fix

Reduce the heading size and let the action remain the focal point. Keep CTA copy direct and proportionate to the surrounding section.

## Common false positives

Campaign pages and editorial statements may intentionally use large display type when the typography is the primary content.
