# Most titles are italic

![Most titles are italic example](./assets/no-mostly-italic-titles.png)

AI-generated websites often italicize most titles instead of building a clear typographic hierarchy.

## How it works

Compares italic headings with all headings and reports when italics dominate.

## How to fix

Use weight, size, spacing, and typeface choice for hierarchy. Reserve italics for occasional emphasis or editorial voice.

## Common false positives

Editorial, literary, and brand-led designs may intentionally use italic display typography.
