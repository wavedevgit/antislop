# Contrasting SVG logo card

![Contrasting SVG logo card example](./assets/no-fake-logo-cards.png)

AI-generated websites often place a generic SVG in a logo card whose color clashes with its parent card.

## How it works

Finds generic SVG marks placed in contrasting rounded logo tiles.
