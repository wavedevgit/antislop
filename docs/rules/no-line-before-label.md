# Line before label

![Line before label example](./assets/no-line-before-label.png)

AI-generated sites often place a short decorative horizontal line immediately before a small label.

## How it works

Finds short decorative horizontal lines immediately before small text labels.

## How to fix

Remove the decorative line. Use spacing, type weight, or alignment to establish the label hierarchy.

## Common false positives

Timelines, legends, and diagrams may use a line as meaningful structure rather than decoration.
