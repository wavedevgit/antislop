# Eyebrows over every title

![Eyebrows over every title example](./assets/no-eyebrows-over-all-titles.png)

AI-generated websites often place smaller, differently colored text above every large bold heading.

## How it works

Counts small accent labels immediately above headings and reports repeated use across most sections.

## How to fix

Remove repeated eyebrow labels. Let section headings carry the hierarchy, keeping a label only when it adds necessary context.

## Common false positives

A single taxonomy label, step name, or legally required category may be useful above a heading.
